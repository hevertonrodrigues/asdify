# Code of Conduct

Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or intimidation. Be respectful, constructive, and specific when criticizing ideas or changes. Do not post personal information without consent.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, or repeated disruptive conduct. Report concerns privately via the repository owner's contact channel once published; until then, do not submit sensitive reports as public issues.

This is a short project-specific policy, not a claim to have adopted another organization's enforcement process.
